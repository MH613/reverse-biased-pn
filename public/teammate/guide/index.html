01 · Equilibrium

Where p meets n, the carriers disappear.

Holes and electrons diffuse across and recombine. They leave fixed ions uncovered: the depletion region.

$$V_{bi}=\frac{kT}{q}\ln\frac{N_AN_D}{n_i^2}=0.714\ \text{V}$$

02 · ρ → E → V

Charge makes the field. The field makes the voltage.

ρ: −qNA, then +qND. Integrate (Gauss): E is a triangle, largest at the junction, pointing n → p. Integrate again: V climbs by Vbi.

$$E_{max}=\frac{qN_Ax_p}{\varepsilon_s}\qquad V_{bi}+V_R=\tfrac12E_{max}W$$

03 · Reverse bias

Pull the junction apart.

p to −, n to +. The battery pulls the carriers away, more ions are uncovered, W widens and the field grows.

$$W\propto\sqrt{V_{bi}+V_R}\qquad E_{max}=\frac{2(V_{bi}+V_R)}{W}$$

06 · Beyond the step

A graded junction bends the triangle.

Doping that changes linearly, ND − NA = a·x: ρ becomes a ramp, E a parabola, and W grows more slowly.

$$W_{abrupt}\propto V^{1/2}\qquad W_{graded}\propto V^{1/3}$$
