The card your phone looks for

Your phone recognises this exact picture and places the 3D junction on it. Print it (any printer, colour or black and white) or just show it on a screen, then open the AR page on your phone.

Scan to open the AR page, then point the phone at the card.
Keep it flat

A bent or creased card tracks worse. Glue it on cardboard if you can.

Good light, no glare

Avoid reflections from lamps on glossy paper or on the screen.

20–40 cm away

The whole card should be in view. Move slowly; it re-locks in a moment if lost.

On a screen?

Turn the brightness up. Laptop, tablet or a second phone all work.
